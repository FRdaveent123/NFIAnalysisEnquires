Thank you for getting in touch with Forest Research about your work. We get many such requests and always do our best to assist. 

We can offer initial advice, direct you to our publicly available data and research, and can sometime recommend useful partner organisations.

If data processing or deeper investigation is required there will usually be a cost, although there is a significant discount for academic work.

General information on the NFI, including what data are collected in the survey, is publicly available on the NFI webpages National Forest Inventory - Forest Research. 

The full survey methodology is described in the NFI Field Survey Guidance, which you may find helpful as background reading NFI Survey Manual for third cycle field samples - Forest Research. 

You might also find these reports useful:
- What our woodlands, and tree cover outside woodlands, are like today; NFI inventory reports and woodland map reports - Forest Research
- How our woodlands might change over time; NFI forecast reports - Forest Research
- NFI customised reports - Forest Research

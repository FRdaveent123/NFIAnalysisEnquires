Thank you for getting in touch with Forest Research about your work. We receive frequent requests for access to NFI data and try to help as far as possible.

Forest Research do not provide precise information on survey locations to protect the statistical integrity of the survey.

For other requests, where heavily de-located location data is appropriate, Forest Research charge staff time, for initial data preparation, and a sliding scale data handling fee based on the number of NFI survey square that are assessed. Data for academic research projects is offered at 75% reduction from standard rate. A contract and data sharing agreement must be agreed and signed.

Currently NFI Cycle1 (2010-2015) and Cycle2 (2015-2020) data is available. NFI Cycle3 data (2020-2025) will be available when data validation and audit is complete.

Please complete the attached data request form describing your project as fully as possible. 

In addition, general information on the NFI, including what data is collected in the survey, is publicly available on the NFI webpages National Forest Inventory - Forest Research. 

The full survey methodology is described in the NFI Field Survey Guidance, which you may find helpful as background reading NFI Survey Manual for third cycle field samples - Forest Research.
